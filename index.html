Wearable display · Concept 01

Wear light.
Write it yourself.

Exovela is a two-layer garment. An inner suit handles your body: heat, sweat, dirt and power. A sealed outer shell is a full display you care for like a phone and never wash. What it shows is up to you: code it in HTML and CSS, describe it to an AI, or wear something made by another creator.

Why light-up clothing faded

The lights were never the problem. The laundry was.

The sound-reactive LED shirts of the 2000s were a hit for a season and then disappeared. They put fragile electronics into a garment that has to be washed, sweated in and folded. Panels cracked, batteries had to be pulled out, and once the novelty wore off, nobody wanted the hassle.

Exovela separates the two jobs a garment does. The part that touches your skin gets washed. The part that shows light never does. Once those two are apart, a garment can carry a real display.

Two layers, two jobs

Built like a spacesuit.

You put the inner suit on first, then the shell. Each layer does its own work, and they connect at a single sealed dock.

LAYER 01 · INNER SUIT

Handles the body

A soft, breathable base layer that manages heat, moisture and dirt. It carries the battery and the controller, and it is the part you wash or swap. It also keeps your body heat away from the display.

  • Wicking, washable knit with ventilated channels
  • Battery and controller in a removable spine pack
  • Thermal spacer between your body and the shell
LAYER 02 · OUTER SHELL

Shows the light

A thin, flexible display skin sealed under a clear protective layer. It is designed to be waterproof, resistant to everyday spills and scratches, and smooth enough that lint and fibers don't stick. You care for it like a phone: wipe it, store it and charge it. You never wash it.

  • Flexible display panels in a sealed laminate
  • No exposed contacts and no seams that trap water
  • Stays good-looking with the display off

Open like Linux

A garment you can program.

Every Exovela skin is a small web page. If you write HTML and CSS, you already know how to design one. If you don't, describe what you want and the AI designer builds it for a few credits. Nothing is locked: you can open, fork and remix any skin you're allowed to.

HTML / CSS / SVG

Standard web code in a sandbox, with no scripts or network access by default.

AI designer

Describe it in words, refine it and wear it. Each generation costs credits.

Base settings

Brightness, motion speed, idle look and a battery saver, all adjustable.

Safety checks

Flash-rate limits and content review run before any skin goes live.

The platform

Express it. Share it. Get paid for it.

The garment works like a console and the skins work like its games. Creators publish skins, owners buy or remix them, and every sale pays the creator. The same designs can also be printed onto physical goods on demand.
