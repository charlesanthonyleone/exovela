Exovela · Concept 01

The outer layer is a display.

Exovela is two pieces. A washable inner suit carries the battery and handles your body heat. Over it goes a sealed, flexible display shell that plays full-motion content, and is wiped clean, never washed.

Open the studio How it’s built

The problem

Light-up clothing never survived the laundry.

The sound-reactive shirts of the mid-2000s put a fragile panel inside a garment that had to be washed, with a battery in a pocket and a graphic fixed at the factory. People wore them for a season.

Exovela separates the two jobs. The inner suit is the part that gets sweaty and goes in the machine, once the spine pack is unclipped. The shell carries the display, is sealed like a phone, and is cared for like one.

Concept render
INNER SUIT

Washable, and carries the power

A wicking knit with spacer-mesh channels that hold the shell off your body and move warm air out. The battery and controller sit in a removable spine pack between the shoulder blades.

DOCK

One sealed connection

Power and data cross at a single magnetic dock on the upper back. Spring pins on the suit side meet flush, potted pads on the shell, and stay unpowered until the two are properly mated.

DISPLAY SHELL

Sealed, never washed

Flexible display tiles follow the pattern pieces under a clear, hard-coated film. No exposed contacts and no seams that hold water. Wipe it, hang it on its charger.

What it plays

Anything you can put on a screen, cut to fit a garment.

Motion pieces, a still, a short you have the rights to, or a page about you that you update like a profile. Skins are written in HTML and CSS, generated from a description, or bought from the people who make them.

Roadmap

Prove the system first. Then make it thin.

01

Hoodie and jacket

First prototypes. Thickness is acceptable. The goal is to prove the two-layer system on a body.

02

Thinner shells

More of the surface covered, better heat handling, less weight.

03

More cuts

A straight jacket, a suit jacket and other base shapes, each with its own shell.

04

Pants

Fixed-cut trousers and joggers in large color zones you set from your phone.
