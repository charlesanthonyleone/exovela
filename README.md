# Exovela

A two-layer wearable display garment, and the site that explains it.

- **Inner suit:** a washable base layer that handles body heat, moisture and dirt, and carries the battery and controller.
- **Outer shell:** a sealed, flexible display skin, waterproof and wipe-clean like a phone, that is never laundered.
- **Platform:** skins written in HTML/CSS (or generated by AI with credits), a creator marketplace, and print on demand.

Invention by Charles Anthony Leone. Concept stage: no product exists, and the garments on the site are rendered live in the browser. **No patent application has been filed.** The proof-of-concept page is an invention disclosure draft for review by a registered patent attorney, not legal advice.

## Pages
- `index.html`: overview
- `how-it-works.html`: the five-layer stack, power, heat, data and care
- `studio.html`: live studio demo (sandboxed HTML/CSS skins on a hoodie, jacket or joggers)
- `marketplace.html`: marketplace, licenses, credits, print on demand
- `roadmap.html`: hoodie and jacket prototypes, then lighter shells, more cuts, and pants
- `proof-of-concept.html`: technical disclosure draft

## Build
Page bodies live in `src/`. Run `node tools/build.mjs` to wrap them with the shared header, pier scene and footer.

## Credits
The hero is an original neon LED pier, rendered in the browser. Stars and moon are procedural; no stock photographs are used. Colored stars are drawn as they might appear without atmosphere or ozone filtering and scattering their light.
