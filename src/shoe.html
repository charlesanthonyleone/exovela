A sneaker that lights up on six sides.

Sealed LED zones on the outer side, inner side, heel, toe cap, tongue and along the sole, and an inner bootie that comes out so you can wash it. It runs everything the sweater does, laid out for a foot that bends.

Prototype resolution: about 5 mm between LEDs. The exact pitch of the first pair isn't settled yet.

Why it's built this way

Wear it in the rain. Wash the part that touches you.

REMOVABLE INNER

A bootie that comes out

The part that touches your foot is a separate knit liner with no electronics in it. Pull it out and put it in the wash.

SEALED LEDS

Waterproof panels

The LED panels are sealed in clear silicone inside the outer shell, so rain and puddles don't reach them. The shell wipes clean.

ONE ACCOUNT

Matches what you're wearing

Same looks, same ad slot, same phone app as the sweater. Pick a look once and both pieces follow it.

Build plan

A first pair from small parts.

Not built yet. These are the parts we'd start with, and every number is an estimate until we measure it.

Full build plan
Side panels
Small flexible boards of tiny addressable LEDs, about 5 mm apart, potted in clear silicone. These would be made to order for the first pair
Heel
A small curved panel of the same LEDs
Toe, tongue, sole
Short LED strips. The sole strip sits behind a translucent band in the midsole. Nothing lights the crease behind the toe cap, where the shoe bends most
Brain
A thumb-sized microcontroller with Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and a motion sensor, in a sealed pod in the heel
Battery
A small rechargeable cell in the heel pod, charged by a magnetic connector or a wireless pad. About 2 to 3 hours at night brightness (estimate)
Liner
Removable knit bootie with no electronics, machine washable
Parts
About $190 to $260 per pair for a hand-built prototype, most of it the custom LED boards (estimate)
