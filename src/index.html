Why light-up clothing faded

Those shirts asked one piece of fabric to get washed and to carry a screen.

The sound-reactive LED shirts of the mid-2000s put a fragile panel in a garment that had to go through the wash. Batteries lived in a pocket. The graphic was whatever left the factory. People loved them for a season and then stopped bothering.

Exovela splits the work across two pieces that attach at a sealed dock. The inner suit is the one that gets dirty and goes in the laundry. The outer shell holds the display, and you maintain it the way you maintain a phone.

Two layers, two jobs

Built like a spacesuit.

You put the inner suit on first, then the shell. They line up on magnets, meet at a sealed pogo-pin dock, and can be pulled apart in one motion. Each piece has its own job.

LAYER 01 · INNER SUIT

Handles the body

A soft, breathable base layer that manages heat, moisture and dirt. It carries the battery and the controller, and it is the part you wash or swap. It also keeps your body heat away from the display.

  • Wicking, washable knit with ventilated channels
  • Battery and controller in a removable spine pack
  • Thermal spacer between your body and the shell
LAYER 02 · OUTER SHELL

Shows the light

A thin, flexible display skin sealed under a clear protective layer. It is designed to be waterproof, resistant to everyday spills and scratches, and smooth enough that lint and fibers don't stick. You care for it like a phone: wipe it, store it and charge it. You never wash it.

  • Flexible display panels in a sealed laminate
  • No exposed contacts and no seams that trap water
  • Stays good-looking with the display off

Open like Linux

A garment you can program.

Every skin is a small web page, or a video, or a still. Write it in HTML and CSS, describe it and spend a few credits, or play a show you have the rights to. People will put a cartoon on a Tuesday and a quiet painting on a Friday. The shell is yours, the way a profile used to be yours.

HTML / CSS / SVG

Standard web code in a sandbox, with no scripts or network access by default.

AI designer

Describe it in words, refine it and wear it. Each generation costs credits.

Base settings

Brightness, motion speed, idle look and a battery saver, all adjustable.

Safety checks

Flash-rate limits and content review run before any skin goes live.

The platform

Express it. Share it. Get paid for it.

Think of it as a profile you wear, and a place where people publish what goes on it. Creators list skins and clips, owners collect, remix and share them, and every sale pays the person who made it. The same work can be printed onto ordinary goods when someone wants a still they can hold.
