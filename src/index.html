The idea

Your clothes become a screen you control.

Most of what we wear says one thing for as long as we own it. Exovela pieces change with the day. A flannel at lunch, a fireplace at a winter party, the second half of the game on your stomach while you talk to friends.

The display hides inside the garment and diffuses through the knit or the shell, so the piece still looks like normal clothing when it's off. Looks are software. You buy the piece once, then download, design or sell what plays on it.

How it works

Zones, a phone, and layouts that move with you.

ZONES

Each area is its own screen

A sweater has a chest, stomach, back, two sleeves and cuffs. The shoe has six zones of its own. Every zone can play something different, or several can join into one picture.

MOTION-AWARE

Built for a body that moves

Pictures stay where the garment holds still: the torso, the side of the shoe. Sleeves get stripes, tickers and widgets. The toe cap creases with every step, so it only ever gets color.

YOUR PHONE

The remote is the app you already carry

Pick a look, mirror your screen, type a message, draw, or run code you wrote. Other people can join a game from their own phones.

SHOWS

Host the things you love

Play episodes of anything you have the rights to. People watch while they talk to you.

WASHABLE

Electronics come out

The sweater's panels sit in pockets of a removable inner layer. The shoe's panels are sealed in silicone, and the bootie that touches your foot has no electronics at all.

SAFE BY DEFAULT

Checked before it plays

Every look and every line of code is checked for flash rate and brightness before it reaches a garment. Stealth turns everything dark in one tap.

Platform and market

Buy the piece once. Change it every day.

THE BASE PIECE

Hardware people keep

The sweater and the shoe are the products you buy. Looks are software, so one piece covers many outfits, and it gets new ones without a new purchase.

CREATOR MARKET

Design, sell and buy looks

A marketplace for looks, widgets and games. Creators set their price, and every listing is checked for fit, power and flash safety first.

How creators would sell

AD SLOTS

Space that follows the wearer

An ad slot belongs to the wearer's account and appears in the same place on every piece they put on. The wearer chooses who rents it and keeps a share.

How the money would work is still a plan: hardware sales, a cut of marketplace sales, and a share of ad slot rentals. We haven't tested prices or demand yet, so there are no market numbers on this page.

Both flagships from parts on sale today.

PROTOTYPE 01 · SWEATER
Chest, stomach, back
Flexible LED matrix panels (2.5 mm pitch) in pockets under the knit
Sleeves and cuffs
Flexible LED strips that bend with the arm, and addressable LED bands
Controller
A small single-board computer for the panels, a microcontroller for sleeves and cuffs, phone app over Wi-Fi
Power
USB-C power bank in the washable inner layer. About 1.5 hours at night brightness (estimate)
Parts
About $950 for one hand-built sweater (estimate)
PROTOTYPE 02 · SHOE
Side panels and heel
Small flexible boards of tiny addressable LEDs about 5 mm apart, potted in clear silicone
Toe, tongue, sole
Short LED strips. The sole strip sits behind a translucent band in the midsole
Brain
A thumb-sized microcontroller with Bluetooth and Wi-Fi and a motion sensor, sealed in a heel pod
Power
A small rechargeable cell in the heel pod. About 2 to 3 hours at night brightness (estimate)
Parts
About $190 to $260 per hand-built pair, most of it the custom LED boards (estimate)

Roadmap and price

Expensive first, then within reach.

Hand-built prototypes cost what the parts cost. The path to a price people pay for good outerwear is custom LED tiles made in volume, fewer and smarter zones on the entry line, and electronics you move between seasons.

Custom tiles

Sealed LED tiles cut to the pattern pieces and made in volume. Lighter, and cheaper per piece.

STEP 3

Within reach

An entry line with fewer zones, and a core you move from your winter sweater to your spring shoes.
