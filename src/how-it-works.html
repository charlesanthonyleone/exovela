How it works

Two pieces, one connection.

The inner suit does everything a garment has to do against skin, including going in the wash. The shell does one thing: show light. They meet at a single sealed dock on the upper back.

The pieces

What each layer is for.

Inner suit · 12
Moisture-wicking knit (polyester or merino with elastane) with 3D spacer-mesh zones. It is the only layer that touches skin, and it goes in the machine once the spine pack is unclipped.
Spine pack · 34
Battery under 100 Wh with a battery-management system, the controller, radio and sensors, in a padded pocket with a thermal barrier on the body side. Charges over USB-C.
Dock · 28 / 94
The only electrical path between the pieces. Spring-loaded pins, magnets and a gasket on the suit side; flush, potted pads on the shell side.
Display shell · 14
Flexible display tiles laid out on the garment's pattern pieces, sealed between a clear hard-coated film and a rear moisture barrier, bonded to a structural textile that gives the shell its drape.

The dock

Sealed on the shell side, dead until mated.

The shell has no openings. Its contact pads are flush and potted, so it can be wiped down or rained on. The suit side carries the moving parts: spring pins, magnets that pull the two into register, an alignment pin and a gasket around the contacts.

The controller only switches the pins on after it confirms a keyed, seated connection, and a sharp tug pulls the dock apart rather than tearing fabric. An inductive version with no exposed metal on either side is an alternative.

Power, heat, care

The hard parts, stated plainly.

POWER

Runtime depends on the content

A dark, slow skin draws a fraction of what bright full-screen video does. Every skin gets a power rating before it can be listed, and brightness has hard caps.

HEAT

Two heat sources, kept apart

Spacer mesh holds an air gap between body and shell and vents warm air at the underarms, sides and hem. The controller dims the display if the shell runs warm.

CARE

Wash one, wipe the other

Unclip the spine pack, wash the inner suit. Wipe the shell with a damp cloth and hang it on its charging hanger. The shell is never laundered.
