Economics

Credits and payouts.

Credits

Credits pay for AI generations and refinements. Owners get a starter balance with each garment, can buy more, and can earn them by selling. Credits never expire mid-project.

Creator share

The proposed starting point is that most of every sale goes to the creator, with a platform fee covering checks, hosting and payments. The exact split is still open and will be modeled before launch.

Remix royalties

When a licensed remix sells, the original creator automatically receives a share. Chains of remixes split along the chain.

Print on demand

Digital first. Physical too.

Print on demand runs on two tracks. The first prints and finishes shells to order: base shell colors and textures, printed trims, and the parts of a shell that don't light up. The second puts any skin a creator allows onto ordinary goods like tees, mugs, posters and phone cases, made only when someone orders.

  • No inventory: every item is made when it's ordered
  • The creator earns on physical goods the same way as on digital sales
  • Animated skins export to a still "poster frame" for printing
  • Fulfillment handled by established print-on-demand partners

Trust and safety

A public screen needs public rules.

Flash safety

Skins that flash faster than the photosensitive-safety threshold of three flashes per second are held back.

Content review

Automatic and human review before listing, with reporting tools for anything that slips through.

Context limits

Brightness ceilings at night, and no patterns that imitate emergency-vehicle lights.
